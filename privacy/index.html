Groceri Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

Groceri is a shopping app that keeps your trips, items, photos and pantry on your own device and in your own iCloud account. This policy describes everything the app collects and everything it sends anywhere.

Everything happens on your device, except Scan Item

Your trips, items, prices, photos, checklists and pantry are stored on your device and sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. There is no Groceri account and no sign-in. Groceri makes no network request of its own until you tap Scan Item.

What happens when you scan a price tag

When you tap Scan Item, frame a price tag and fire the shutter, the cropped photo is sent to Groceri's own server, which passes it to OpenAI to read the name and price off the tag. The reading comes back to your device and is filled into the Add Item sheet for you to check.

Groceri keeps nothing. The photo and the reading exist on Groceri's server only for the seconds the request is in flight. They are never written to storage and never logged, on success or on failure. OpenAI keeps the photo no longer than 30 days for abuse monitoring, processes it in the United States, and does not use it to train its models.

The photo itself stays on your device as the item's photo, like any other photo you add.

The anonymous identifier that enforces the scan allowance

Scanning is free and metered. To count scans against one installation of the app, Groceri's server stores an anonymous identifier generated on your device by Apple's App Attest, together with the count of scans used and when the count resets. That identifier belongs to one installation of Groceri. It is not linked to you, to your name, to your Apple Account, to your iCloud data or to any purchase, it is not shared with anyone, and it is deleted after 90 days without a scan. Groceri's server never stores your IP address, and its logs record only the outcome of a request, never the identifier.

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

Groceri contains no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits that collect data. Nothing about you or your shopping is ever sold or shared with data brokers.

Children

Groceri has no accounts and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy lives in Groceri's source repository next to the code it describes, so every change to it is a public, reviewable change. The date at the top is the date it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/luojiahai/Groceri/issues.
