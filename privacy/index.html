Groceri Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Groceri is a shopping app that keeps your trips, items, photos and pantry on your own device and in your own iCloud account. This policy describes everything the app collects and everything it sends anywhere.

Everything happens on your device, except scanning and buying scans

Your trips, items, prices, photos, checklists and pantry are stored on your device and sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. There is no Groceri account and no sign-in. The app calls a server of its own for five things: getting the short-lived pass it needs before it can call at all, reading a price tag when you tap Scan Item, showing how many scans you have left when you open the Me tab, recording scans you buy, and restoring your purchases when you ask it to. Everything else that leaves your device goes to Apple, for iCloud sync and for the App Store.

What happens when you scan a price tag

When you tap Scan Item, frame a price tag and fire the shutter, the cropped photo is sent to Groceri's own server, which passes it to OpenAI to read the name and price off the tag. The reading comes back to your device and is filled into the Add Item sheet for you to check.

Groceri keeps nothing. The photo and the reading exist on Groceri's server only for the seconds the request is in flight. They are never written to storage and never logged, on success or on failure. OpenAI keeps the photo no longer than 30 days for abuse monitoring, processes it in the United States, and does not use it to train its models.

The photo itself stays on your device as the item's photo, like any other photo you add.

The identifier that carries your scan balance

Scanning is metered. Your first 20 scans are free, and after that you can buy more scans or subscribe. To keep the count, Groceri's server stores your balance under an identifier Apple issues for your Apple Account. That identifier is not your name and not your email address, and everything the server knows under it is listed in this section. The record is opened the first time the app talks to the server, whether you ever buy anything or not.

Apple takes the payment. Groceri never sees your card, your name or your address, and never asks for them. What reaches Groceri's server is a receipt Apple signed, naming what was bought.

For each purchase the server keeps what was bought, Apple's transaction number for it, how many scans it credited, and the date of a refund if one is ever made. Alongside those it keeps your balance, how many of your first 20 scans are left and when they were issued, whether a subscription is running and when it expires, and a running count of the scans and the failed reads from your last 24 hours.

Apple also tells Groceri's server directly when you buy something, when a purchase is refunded, and when a subscription renews, lapses or is cancelled. Those messages are signed by Apple, name the same Apple Account identifier, and describe the purchase they are about. They are how your balance stays right while the app is closed.

If a purchase is refunded, the server takes back the scans that purchase credited. If you had already spent them, your balance goes below zero, and the scans you owe come out of the next scans you buy before you can use them.

That identifier is never deleted. A balance you paid for has to outlive deleting the app and reinstalling it, and the same record is what stops the 20 free scans being claimed over and over.

Groceri's server asks Apple about your own purchases, using the identifier Apple issued for them. Apple is the only place your purchases and your balance ever go. None of it is sold, and none of it is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

The anonymous identifier that proves the app is genuine

Groceri's server has to know it is talking to the real Groceri app on a real device, and not to something pretending to be it. Your device generates an anonymous identifier through Apple's App Attest, and Groceri's server stores it with the public key and counter that go with it. It is not linked to you, to your name, to your iCloud data or to anything you have bought.

The server touches it only when the app asks for a new pass, which the app needs before it can scan, read your balance, record a purchase or restore one. Ninety days after the last time the app asked, the server deletes it and everything stored with it.

It is never stored beside the Apple Account identifier above. The two are never written into the same record and never appear in the same log line. One part of the server sees both for as long as it takes to check them, and it writes neither down.

Groceri's server never reads or stores your IP address. Its logs record what a request was and how it ended, never either identifier.

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

Groceri contains no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits that collect data. Nothing about you or your shopping is ever sold or shared with data brokers.

Children

Groceri has no accounts and no sign-in, and it asks nobody for a name, an email address or a date of birth. What you type into Groceri stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.

Changes to this policy

This policy lives in a public repository, so every change to it is a public, reviewable change. The date at the top is the date it last changed.
